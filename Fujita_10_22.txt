Dear Ehsan,

I understand your proposed two-step approach, but I still have concerns about the central scientific contribution.

Obtaining transition-line labels directly from simulations does not require edge detection in the first place.
The important question is what your method offers compared with conventional image reconstruction followed by line detection.

The previous approach also preserves transition features.
You need to explain what is gained or lost by predicting the lines directly, and why the results are useful for subsequent analysis.
The proposed experimental verification step has not been tested and cannot yet support this claim.

I also find the distinction between simulated charge configurations, simulated sensor signals, and experimental measurements unclear in parts of the manuscript.

My concern is that reviewers will not understand the scientific contribution and reject the paper before its potential value is assessed.
This cannot be resolved by correcting individual statements alone.

I have attached my comments on the earlier version, mainly covering the Abstract and Introduction.
Most remain relevant.

Before another full revision, please reconsider the central claim and send me an independent outline of what has actually been demonstrated, why it matters, and which results support it.
I would like you to develop this argument yourself before we proceed with further detailed review.
